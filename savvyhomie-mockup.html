Questions people ask

What's the best way to organize a small kitchen on a budget?

Start with the drawer you open most. One divider and two bins fix more daily friction than any single big purchase — then expand shelf by shelf.

Are drawer organizers worth it?

Yes, if sized to your drawer. Measure first: the #1 cause of bad reviews is buying fixed-width organizers for odd drawers.
