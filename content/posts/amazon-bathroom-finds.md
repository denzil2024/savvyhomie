## Questions People Ask

<details>
<summary>What if I'm renting and can't drill into the walls?</summary>

Several picks on this list are built for exactly that. The adhesive shower shelf skips drilling entirely, the hanging mesh caddy just hooks over the shower head, and the floating shelves are the only two items here that need real wall anchors. Everything else sits on a counter, a shelf, or the floor.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where should I start in a small bathroom?</summary>

Storage first, decor second. A small bathroom feels the effects of clutter faster than a large one, so the over-the-toilet shelf, the under-sink bins, or the lazy susan turntables solve more of the day-to-day frustration than a candle or a piece of wall art will. Once the storage is handled, one or two decor pieces go a lot further in a room that already feels calmer.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Will any of these hold up in a bathroom with no fan or window?</summary>

The teak shower mat and the reed diffuser are both built with that exact problem in mind, the teak resists water damage on its own, and the diffuser works without needing airflow to carry a scent the way a spray does. The reclaimed wood shelves and wall art hold up fine too, because they're not sitting directly in the shower's splash zone.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What's worth buying first if I can only get a couple things right now?</summary>

The over-the-toilet shelf and the under-sink storage bins solve the two most common complaints, wasted wall space and a chaotic cabinet, and both work in almost any bathroom layout. Everything else on this list is worth adding too, but those two fix problems that show up every single day.

</details>

## Final Thoughts

None of these 27 finds require tearing anything out or calling a contractor. A shelf goes up, a curtain gets swapped, a tray corrals what used to sit loose on the counter, and the room starts feeling less like it's working against whoever uses it every morning. Start with whatever's bothering you the most today and build out from there.
