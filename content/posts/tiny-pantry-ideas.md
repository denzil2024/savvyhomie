<details><summary>How do you create a pantry in a kitchen that doesn't have one?</summary>Repurpose a nearby closet, an unused cabinet, or a blank wall instead of trying to build a new room. A hallway linen closet, a mudroom cubby, or the area under a staircase can all hold food storage without any construction.</details>

<details><summary>What's the best way to store food when there's no floor space left?</summary>Go vertical instead. Shelf risers, stacking bins, and tall narrow towers all add storage without claiming new floor room, since they build up in the same footprint a kitchen already has.</details>

<details><summary>How do you hide open food storage so it doesn't look cluttered?</summary>A curtain rod mounted along the front edge of open shelving, a sliding barn door over a shelving nook, or matching canisters instead of mismatched boxes all disguise storage that has to stay visible. Woven baskets work the same way for produce and packaged snacks.</details>

<details><summary>Can a closet work as a pantry?</summary>Yes, and it's one of the most common fixes for a kitchen with no pantry. A coat closet, linen closet, or even a bedroom closet shelf can hold shelf-stable food once it's fitted with the right shelving or bins.</details>

<details><summary>What can go on the wall if there's no room for a pantry cabinet?</summary>Wire racks, spice rails, pegboard panels, and magnetic knife strips all work as wall-only storage. None of them require floor clearance, which makes them useful in a kitchen too tight for a freestanding cabinet.</details>

<details><summary>Is the space above the fridge or under a bench seat worth using for storage?</summary>Yes, both are commonly wasted in small kitchens. The space above a fridge holds bulk paper goods well with a simple shelf, and a bench seat with a lift-up lid can hide backup supplies without adding new furniture.</details>

## Final Thoughts

None of these fixes require a renovation or a single extra square foot of house. A kitchen with no pantry still has a closet somewhere, a blank wall, a gap beside the fridge, room that already exists and just never got assigned a job. Walk through your own kitchen and name the spot that's doing the least for you right now, whether that's overhead, under a bench, or behind a door, and start there. That's the real pantry your kitchen was hiding the whole time.
