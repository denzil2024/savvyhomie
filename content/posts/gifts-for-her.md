<details open>
<summary>What if I don't know her exact taste?</summary>
<p>Stick to categories built around daily use instead of personal style: loungewear, beauty basics, or a home fragrance pick work for almost anyone. Save picks from Hobbies or the Keepsakes section for someone whose specific interests are already known well.</p>

<details>
<summary>How do I narrow down nine categories to the right one?</summary>
<p>Match the category to how she spends her downtime, not a hobby she mentioned once in passing. Someone who's always doing her nighttime skincare routine needs a different starting point than someone who's mid-puzzle every weekend, and most people fit clearly into two or three of these categories, not all nine.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What's a safe gift if she says she doesn't want anything?</summary>
<p>Consumables and low-commitment picks are the safest option here, candles, skincare minis, cozy socks. They get used up or blend easily into an existing routine instead of sitting around as one more thing to find space for, which is usually what's behind "I don't need anything" in the first place.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How much should I spend?</summary>
<p>This list runs from under $20 (the earbuds, the candle, the earring set) to over $100 (the tote bag, the weighted blanket, the personalized keepsakes), so budget doesn't limit the options much. Let the occasion set the range instead, a casual gift calls for a different pick than a milestone one.</p>
</details>

## Final Thoughts

The best gift here depends on which category matches her, not which pick ranks highest on this list. Someone who unwinds with a hobby every night and someone who's built her whole routine around skincare need completely different picks, and that's fine. Pick two or three from the categories that fit her well rather than trying to cover every base at once.
