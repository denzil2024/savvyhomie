Coworkers, bosses, and neighbors read better in the $15 to $35 range, generous enough to feel thoughtful without crossing into too much for someone seen twice a year.</p>

<details>

</details>

<details>

<p>Often, yes. A few picks here, like the fire pit or the BBQ tool set, work just as well for a brother as they do for a friend. Use the category as a starting point, not a strict rule, and let the actual person guide the final call.</p>

## Final Thoughts

Good gifts for men depend less on the product and more on getting the relationship right first. A dad and a coworker rarely want the same thing, and treating them the same is how a gift list ends up feeling generic. Start with who it's for, then let the category do the rest of the work.
