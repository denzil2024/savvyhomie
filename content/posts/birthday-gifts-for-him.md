## Questions People Ask

<details open>
<summary>How do I decide which category to shop from?</summary>
<p>Start with how well you know him and how big the birthday is. Close relationships and milestone years lean toward sentimental or splurge picks, while a coworker's birthday or a smaller celebration usually calls for something from the practical or fun and novelty categories instead.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I mix categories in one gift?</summary>
<p>Yes, pairing works well here. A practical or comfort pick alongside something small from the fun and novelty section covers both the useful and the memorable without needing to spend splurge-level money.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What if he says he doesn't want anything?</summary>
<p>That's usually when an experience gift works best. It sidesteps the "I don't need more stuff" objection since it's not adding to a pile of things, it's giving him something to do.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Are the splurge picks worth it for a smaller birthday?</summary>
<p>Not usually. Save those for milestone years or when the budget comfortably allows for it. For a regular birthday, the practical, comfort, or fun and novelty categories deliver more relative to what they cost.</p>
</details>

## Final Thoughts

Birthday gifts for him work better when the gift type matches the moment instead of defaulting to whatever's easiest to find. A coworker's birthday and a close friend's milestone year call for completely different categories, and shopping by type instead of by hobby makes that distinction easier to act on.
