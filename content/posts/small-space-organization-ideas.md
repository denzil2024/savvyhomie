## FAQ

<details>
<summary>Will an over-door organizer fit a thicker interior door?</summary>
Interior over-door racks are typically rated for doors up to about 1.75 inches thick, which covers nearly every interior door in a US home. Measure first if the door is a solid slab or has a raised panel, because either can change the actual thickness.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How much weight can a lift-top coffee table's storage well hold?</summary>
Lift-top storage wells are generally rated for 20 to 30 pounds, enough for blankets or board games but not a stack of hardcover books. Check the specific listing's weight rating before loading it up.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Do breathable garment bags work for leather or suede coats?</summary>
Yes, because the material needs to breathe rather than sit sealed. Skip vacuum-sealed plastic bags for leather or suede specifically, because the compression can crack or dry out the surface over time.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Can an under-sink pullout drawer fit around plumbing?</summary>
These are commonly built with an adjustable or split design meant to work around a P-trap and supply lines, not a totally open cabinet. Measure the gap on either side of the pipes before assuming a fixed-width drawer will clear them.
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is a valet stand worth it, or does a closet rod work fine?</summary>
A valet stand earns its keep for next-day outfits specifically, because pulling one full look out of a packed rod means it gets wrinkled or knocked to the floor. For everything else, a regular rod works fine.
</details>
