Christmas gifts for coworkers walk a tight line: thoughtful enough to not feel like an afterthought, but not so personal it's awkward to receive from someone you see forty hours a week. The 27 picks below stay on the right side of that line, organized by desk essentials, drinkware, treats, comfort, stationery, and fun extras.

Shopping for more than one person this year? The [45 Christmas Gift Ideas](/blog/christmas-gift-ideas/) guide covers everyone else on the list, and the [24 Gifts for Wine Lovers](/blog/gifts-for-wine-lovers/) guide is worth a look if one coworker in particular is a wine person.

## Questions People Ask

<details open>
<summary>How much should I spend on a coworker gift?</summary>
<p>Most coworker gifts land between $15 and $25, enough to feel thoughtful without creating pressure for the other person to reciprocate at the same level. Save anything pricier for someone you're already close with outside of work.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Is it okay to give the same gift to the whole team?</summary>
<p>Yes, and it's often the safer choice. A matching bulk gift, like the socks or the snack box above, avoids the awkwardness of one person clearly getting a nicer gift than everyone else.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What if I don't want it to seem like I'm trying too hard?</summary>
<p>Stick to practical, useful picks over anything sentimental, a desk item or a snack box reads as thoughtful without reading as a grand gesture. Save the more personal picks, like the photo keychain or the ornament, for someone you already know well outside of work.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>Should I get my boss something too?</summary>
<p>Check the office norms first, some workplaces actively discourage gifting up the chain to avoid the appearance of favor-currying. If it's normal where you work, keep it in the same $15 to $25 range as coworker gifts, nothing that reads as trying to impress.</p>
</details>

## Final Thoughts

A good coworker gift doesn't need to say much. It just needs to land somewhere between thoughtful and low-key. Pick something useful over something sentimental unless you already know the relationship can handle it, and the rest takes care of itself.
