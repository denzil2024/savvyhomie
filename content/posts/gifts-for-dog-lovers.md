Every dog lover already owns a paw-print mug and a "Dog Mom" tumbler somewhere in a cabinet. The trick with gifts for dog lovers is finding something that gets used instead of stored away. This list sticks to that: gear for daily walks, comfort items for the dog itself, and a few keepsakes worth keeping.

It's organized by walking gear, home comfort, keepsakes, apparel, and treats below, so it's easy to jump to whichever category fits the dog lover on the list.

## Questions People Ask

<details open>
<summary>Should a gift for a dog lover be for them or for their dog?</summary>
<p>Either works, and the best lists usually mix both. Items like the coffee mug or the enamel pins are for the person, while the puzzle toy and the freeze-dried treats are for the dog, and a few, like the coffee and treats basket, cover both at once.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>How much should I spend on a gift for a dog lover?</summary>
<p>Most of the picks on this list land between $10 and $40, depending on whether it's a small stocking-stuffer style gift or something bigger like the orthopedic bed or the gift basket. Match the spend to how close the relationship is, not to how much someone loves their dog.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary>What if I don't know their dog's breed or size?</summary>
<p>Stick to items that don't depend on breed or size: the treat pouch, the coffee mug, the enamel pins, and the hair roller all work regardless. Anything sized to a specific dog, like the harness or the elevated bowl stand, is safer to skip without knowing more.</p>
</details>
